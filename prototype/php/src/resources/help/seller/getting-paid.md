Every sale is held in escrow the moment the buyer pays. It releases to your balance, awaiting payout, when the buyer confirms delivery.

Payout periods run Monday to Sunday. Everything released by Sunday night is paid on the Monday. A refund after payout is carried forward and netted against your next payout.

The platform takes a fee out of each sale's item subtotal. Your earnings and each statement show the fee for that sale.
