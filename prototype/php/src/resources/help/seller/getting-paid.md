Every sale is held in escrow the moment the buyer pays. It releases to your available balance when the buyer confirms delivery, or seven days after the carrier marks the parcel delivered, whichever comes first.

Payout periods run Monday to Sunday. Everything released by Sunday night is paid on the Monday, to the account on your Earnings page. A refund after payout is carried forward and netted against your next payout.

The platform fee is 10% of the item subtotal. Shipping you charge the buyer is passed through in full.
