title: Printing a label from an order
slug: printing-a-label-from-an-order
position: 1
---

Open the order and complete a step your flow has set to print a label. That step asks for a carrier and tracking number, records both, and hands you the printable label page. A flow can set more than one step to print a label.

Complete the rest of your flow's steps as the parcel moves; each completion shows on the order's activity feed, with the carrier and tracking number a label step recorded. Mark shipped, beside your flow's steps, is the separate control that tells the buyer their parcel is on its way.

Your steps are yours. Flow settings, linked from any order, is where you add, rename, reorder, and remove them, and where you choose which ones print a label.
