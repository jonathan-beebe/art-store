Open the order and complete your flow's label step. A step that prints a label asks for a carrier and tracking number, records both, and hands you the printable label page.

Complete the rest of your flow's steps as the parcel moves; each completion is recorded against the order. Marking the order shipped is its own step, and it is the one that tells the buyer their parcel is on its way.

Your steps are yours. Flow settings, linked from any order, is where you add, rename, reorder, and remove them, and where you choose which one prints the label.
