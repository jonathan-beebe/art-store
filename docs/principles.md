# Principles of Digital Product Stewardship

- Optimize for accessibility, strictly adhering to WCAG guidelines. The goal is to maximize the number of humans who can effectively use this platform for selling and buying art.
- Iterative workflow. We work iteratively, shipping early, validating, and growing features.
- Test-centric development process that validates and protects expected behaviors with unit and integration tests. This is what enables us to iterate fast, since the test suite becomes our assurance we can't break expected behaviors.
- All flows, including error flows, are expected behavior. Errors will happen. Every error needs to route to a well defined and predictable user flow so that the human experiencing the error knows what happened and what they can or should do. If the workflow can be retried, we render a retry-able UX. If the error means wait, we tell the human to wait. If the error is catastrophic, like platform downtime, we let the human know. Never leave the human guessing.
